Zelda: Inside The Dream

Zelda: Inside The Dream lets you play The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (2019) in first person, in VR on PC through SteamVR, or on the screen on Windows, Linux and Mac. It is built on LAS Recomp by Peiikai.

Install it.

Inside The Dream doesn't include the game, and there is nothing from Nintendo in the downloads. You need your own copy of Link's Awakening for Nintendo Switch, as its extracted files: the exefs and romfs folders. We play and test with version 1.0.0, and versions 1.0.1 and 1.1.0 are accepted too. On the first launch, the app rebuilds the game's code for your own computer, once: it takes about seven minutes on a recent desktop PC, and it needs about 12 GB free.

On Windows and Linux, the game needs a 64-bit processor with AVX2 (Intel 4th generation or newer, or AMD Ryzen), a graphics card with Vulkan 1.3 and 8 GB of RAM. On a Mac, it needs Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 15.

Windows

You run the installer, which puts an Inside The Dream shortcut on your desktop, or you unzip the zip and open lasrecomp.exe. On the first launch, you click Choose the folder, then Set up the game. To play in VR, you start SteamVR first, then you click PLAY IN VR.

Linux

You make the AppImage executable and open it, then you click Choose the folder and Set up the game. On the Steam Deck, you add it to Steam as a non-Steam game.

Mac

You open the .dmg and drag Inside The Dream into Applications. The first time, macOS may refuse it: you click Open Anyway in System Settings, Privacy & Security.

The controls.

You look around with the right stick or the mouse. R3 switches between the first-person view and the game's own view from above, and L3 or F5 puts the camera behind Link and back. In the headset, a long press on the left menu button opens the menu on the floating screen.
